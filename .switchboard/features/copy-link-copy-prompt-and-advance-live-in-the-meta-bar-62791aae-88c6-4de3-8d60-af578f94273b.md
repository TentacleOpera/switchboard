# Copy Link, Copy Prompt and Advance Live in the Meta-Bar

**Complexity:** 5

## Goal

Put document actions where users look for them - the meta-bar above the preview, not buried in a sidebar card's action row - across all four doc-type panels. Copy Link establishes the pattern, Copy Prompt and Advance follow it into the kanban plans tab, and the button that advances a card is made to feel like a press instead of flashing green a second after the click with its label rewritten.

## How the Subtasks Achieve This

- **Relocate Copy Link to preview meta-bars across all panels** — establishes the meta-bar pattern across the planning, project, tickets and design panels, moving the action out of sidebar cards where users do not look for it.
- **Add Copy Prompt and Advance buttons to the project.html kanban plans meta-bar** — follows that pattern for the two column-advancement actions; it cites the Copy Link plan directly.
- **Copy-prompt button copied feedback fires late and flashes green** — makes the feedback fire on click as a subtle press, with no label rewrite and no flash a second after the card has already moved.
